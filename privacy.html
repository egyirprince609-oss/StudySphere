Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Code Academy may collect, use, store and share information when you visit the public website or use account-based learning features.

Last updated: 25 October 2026Public page · No sign-in required

1. Overview

Code Academy is an educational application that helps learners study coding and use related learning tools. The public information pages, including this policy, are intended to be accessible without signing in. Account-based learning features may require authentication.

We aim to collect and use information only for purposes connected to operating, protecting and improving the learning service. This page must accurately reflect the actual app and its live integrations; please review it whenever data practices change.

2. Information that may be handled

Depending on which features you use and which options you choose, Code Academy may handle the following categories:

  • Account and sign-in information: a Firebase user identifier, email address, display name, profile image URL and sign-in provider information returned by the authentication method you choose.
  • Learning and profile information: course or lesson progress, quiz and challenge activity, points or levels, notes, saved code, preferences and other information you choose to save through the app.
  • Content you submit: questions, text, code snippets, prompts, images or other materials that you deliberately submit to a tutor, generator or other feature.
  • Basic technical information: limited service or error information may be processed to troubleshoot, protect and maintain the app, depending on the hosting and monitoring configuration.

You can browse the public homepage, privacy policy, terms, usage policy and documentation without providing account information.

3. Google sign-in and Google user data

If you choose “Sign in with Google” or another Google authentication option, Google may share the basic profile information you authorise, commonly your name, email address, profile picture and an account identifier. The exact information depends on the permissions displayed during sign-in.

Code Academy uses this information to authenticate you, identify your account, display relevant profile details, maintain account access and support the features you request. We do not ask for access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or other Google services as part of basic sign-in unless the app separately explains and requests a relevant permission.

Google account data is not used to serve advertising, sold to data brokers, or disclosed to AI providers merely because you sign in. It is shared only where necessary to operate authentication and the app, protect users and the service, comply with law, or where you direct or consent to a specific action. Google data is not used for unrelated purposes or to train general-purpose AI models.

Google handles authentication under its own terms and privacy policy. Review Google's Privacy Policy for more information. Code Academy requests only the sign-in information needed for the functions described above.

4. How information may be used

Information may be used to create and maintain your account, show your profile, save the learning activity you request, display progress, provide app features, respond to support requests, diagnose errors, prevent misuse and meet legal obligations. Information may also be used to remember preferences and make the experience easier to continue across supported sessions.

We do not claim that every feature stores all activity. What is saved depends on the feature and the current application implementation.

5. Sharing and service providers

Code Academy may use service providers to deliver necessary functions, such as Firebase Authentication, Firestore or other Firebase services, hosting, and selected AI or media APIs. Those providers process information as needed to perform their services under their own terms and privacy documentation. Only information necessary for the relevant function should be sent to a provider.

We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed where required by law, to address abuse or security threats, to protect rights and safety, or during a legitimate change to the service, subject to applicable law. This policy does not grant third parties permission to use information for their own unrelated purposes.

6. AI, code, image and voice features

When you submit a prompt to an AI tutor, maths helper, question generator, image generator, speech feature or a similar optional tool, the text, code, image or audio you submit may be sent to the provider powering that feature so it can generate a response. The specific provider depends on the current configuration.

Do not include passwords, private keys, financial details, personal records or confidential information in prompts. Do not submit another person's personal information without an appropriate basis and permission. AI-generated outputs may be inaccurate. Review the provider's own privacy terms for details about how that provider processes submitted content.

Google sign-in data is not intentionally included in AI prompts or transferred to AI providers just to generate answers. If you manually paste personal or account information into a prompt, that information may be processed as part of the prompt.

7. Storage and security

Account and learning data may be stored using Firebase services configured for the application. Technical and operational protections depend on the deployed configuration, including authentication settings, database access rules, backend permissions and secret management. We aim to use reasonable safeguards, but no internet service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Private API keys and service credentials should be kept on the server or in protected environment settings, never in public frontend code or a public source repository. Users should protect their own devices and account access details.

8. Retention and deletion

Information may be retained while an account is active or for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes or meet legal requirements. Exact retention periods can vary by data type and provider.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of account information, contact the developer through the Code Academy project repository. Do not post private data in public issues. A request may need verification. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete information already stored on a server.

9. Young learners

Code Academy may be used by students, including young learners. Users should provide only information needed for learning. Where a parent or guardian's consent is required by applicable law or platform rules, use of the relevant account or feature should be supervised and authorised appropriately. Do not use community or AI features to share a child's address, school schedule, phone number or other sensitive personal details.

10. Your choices

  • You can choose whether to use Google sign-in or another supported sign-in method.
  • You can choose whether to submit content to optional AI and media tools.
  • You can avoid submitting personal or confidential information in prompts.
  • You can contact the developer to request review or deletion of account data as described above.

Some information is necessary to authenticate an account or provide a feature you request. If you do not provide it, that feature may not work.

11. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's features, integrations or data practices change. The revision date at the top will be updated after a change. We encourage users to revisit this page periodically.

Privacy questions or data requests can be submitted through the Code Academy GitHub repository. Do not share account passwords, API keys or sensitive personal data in public support posts.

Important: This is a working policy draft prepared from the described app features. Before using it for Google brand verification or production, confirm the live Firebase configuration, actual OAuth scopes, AI providers, data retention and deletion process. The published policy must match actual practice.
